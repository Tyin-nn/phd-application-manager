# Cold Email 套磁邮件模版

> 说明：
> - 套磁信务必短：**150 词左右**，导师没时间读长文。
> - 发送前先查该组主页是否明确写了 "Do not email PIs"（北欧职位制常见，直接走官方渠道即可）。
> - 最佳发送时间：工作日上午（按对方时区）。一周无回复可礼貌 follow up 一次。

---

## 版本 A：有明确职位发布

**Subject:** Prospective PhD applicant — [Position ref number] / [Project title]

Dear Dr. 【Surname】,

I am Your Name, a second-year Master's student at Your University working with Your Supervisor on the star formation rate function of the Kilo-Degree Survey (KiDS DR4), which is being submitted to *Sci. Ch. Phys. Mech. Astron.*

I am very interested in the PhD position 【title, ref no.】 advertised on 【where you found it】. My experience in measuring galaxy statistics from large surveys and comparing them with hydrodynamical simulations (EAGLE/COLIBRE) closely matches the project's focus on 【关键词】.

I have attached my CV and would be happy to provide any further materials. I would greatly appreciate the opportunity to discuss the position with you.

Best regards,
Your Name
M.Sc. candidate, Your University
【email】 | 【phone】

## 版本 B：没有公开职位，主动询问

**Subject:** Prospective PhD student inquiry — galaxy evolution / [topic]

Dear Dr. 【Surname】,

I am Your Name, a second-year Master's student at Your University (supervisor: Your Supervisor). My research focuses on the star formation rate function of KiDS DR4 since z=2, with a paper in preparation.

I read your recent paper on 【具体论文题目】， and I was particularly interested in 【一句具体的话，不要空泛】. I am writing to ask whether you expect to have any PhD openings in 【year】, or whether you would consider supervising a project on 【你的想法，一句话】.

My CV is attached. Thank you very much for your time.

Best regards,
Your Name
【email】

## 注意事项
- [ ] 收件人称呼用 Dr./Prof. + 姓氏，不确定就查组主页
- [ ] 提到对方至少一篇论文的具体内容（版本 B 必须做到）
- [ ] 附件命名规范：`CV_YourName.pdf`
- [ ] 不要群发痕迹（CC 一堆人、明显模板句）
