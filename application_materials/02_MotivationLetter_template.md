# Motivation Letter / Cover Letter 模版

> 说明：
> - 欧洲 PhD 职位申请的动机信一般 **1 页以内**，最多不超过 1.5 页。
> - 结构：开头点明申请职位 → 研究经历与成果 → 为什么是这个组/项目 → 未来规划 → 结尾。
> - 每投一个组都要改【Why this group】部分，切忌群发同一封。

---

Dear Dr. 【Surname】 / Members of the Selection Committee,

I am writing to apply for the PhD position in 【project title, ref number】 at 【University/Institute】. I am currently a second-year Master's student in astrophysics at Your University, working with Your Supervisor on measuring the star formation rate function (SFRF) with the Kilo-Degree Survey (KiDS DR4). I expect to obtain my degree in June 2027 and am available to start from 【July/August/Sep】 2027.

**Research background.** My Master's research focuses on the census of galaxy formation over the last ~10 Gyr. Using KiDS DR4 photometry and 【SED-fitting tool】, I derive star formation rates for tens of thousands of galaxies and construct the SFRF out to z=2, which will be submitted to *Science China Physics, Mechanics & Astronomy*. In addition, I have worked on the cosmic star formation rate density and on the JiuTian hydrodynamical simulation, which gave me hands-on experience with both observational data and simulations. This combination directly matches your project's goal of 【用项目原文的关键词改写：e.g., constraining galaxy evolution with JWST/ALMA surveys】.

**Why this position.** I have followed your work on 【具体论文题目或主题，引用一两篇他们的论文】， particularly 【一句话说这篇论文哪里吸引你/与你工作的联系】. My experience in 【SFRF/SMF measurements, survey data pipelines】 would allow me to contribute to 【项目的具体任务】， while the opportunity to work with 【JWST/ALMA/Euclid 数据、某个模拟】 would push my research toward 【高红移星系、宇宙黎明等你想做的方向】.

Beyond research skills, my supervisor describes me as an organized researcher who delivers clear presentations and well-crafted figures — qualities I believe are essential for collaborative projects like yours.

I would be delighted to discuss my application further and can be available for an online interview at any time. Thank you for considering my application.

Sincerely,
Your Name 【署名用拼音】

---

### 写作要点 Checklist
- [ ] 第一段：职位名称 + ref number + 你是谁 + 何时能入职
- [ ] 第二段：研究经历（数据量、方法、产出），与职位要求逐条对应
- [ ] 第三段：读过对方的论文，说明为什么是“这个”组
- [ ] 全文 ≤ 1 页（约 400–500 词）
- [ ] 无语法错误（可用 Grammarly/LanguageTool 检查后请导师或朋友过一遍）
